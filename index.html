Serving Anglers, Boaters & Travelers on US-90

Located conveniently at 52250 US-90 in Slidell, LA, Rigolets Bait & Seafood provides essential marine supplies, live bait, diesel, and refreshed options right along the water's edge.

Whether you're gearing up for an early morning fishing run in the Rigolets pass, refueling your boat via deep water dock access, or stopping by for hot coffee and Blue Bell ice cream, our doors are open daily to support your journey.
